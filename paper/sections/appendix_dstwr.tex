Let $T_{PS}$, $T_{PR}$, $T_{RS}$ denote the tag's POLL send, POLL\_ACK receive,
and RANGE send timestamps (in DW1000 ticks, corrected by antenna delay).
Let $T_{PR_A}$, $T_{AS}$, $T_{AR}$ denote the anchor's POLL receive,
POLL\_ACK send, and RANGE receive timestamps.
Define the four intervals:
\begin{align*}
  R_1 &= T_{PR}   - T_{PS},   \quad &P_1 = T_{AS} - T_{PR_A}, \\
  R_2 &= T_{AR}   - T_{AS},   \quad &P_2 = T_{RS} - T_{PR}.
\end{align*}

Let $\delta_t$ and $\delta_a$ be the clock-frequency offsets of tag and anchor
relative to the ideal frequency.
The round-trip intervals satisfy
$R_1 = (1+\delta_t)[\tau + \tau + P_1/(1+\delta_a)]$ (to first order).
