\subsection{Limitations}

\textbf{EKF is 2D; Z is solved separately.}
The FusionEKF state is currently 2D (position + velocity + bias in XY).
With five anchors including one elevated, $z$ is geometrically observable
from trilateration, but is not yet a formal EKF state.
Extending to a 9-state 3D EKF is the recommended next step; the host already
outputs $z$ from the LM solver as a scalar sidecar to the EKF fix.

\textbf{Update rate.}
The synchronous-blocking \texttt{sweep()} limits the UWB measurement rate
to $\approx10$\,Hz.
IMU prediction between sweeps decouples position smoothness (IMU, 100\,Hz)
from position accuracy (UWB, 10\,Hz), but closed-loop control demanding
faster position feedback would benefit from asynchronous scheduling.

\textbf{Multi-tag collisions.}
Multiple tags share the channel without coordination.
Collision probability is low for 2--3 tags at 10\,Hz but increases with more.
Full TDMA superframe coordination is required for deterministic multi-tag
scaling beyond three tags.

\subsection{Diagnostics and Known Issues}

\textbf{6.8 Mbps mode failure.}
We attempted to switch to \texttt{MODE\_SHORTDATA\_FAST\_ACCURACY}
(6.8\,Mbps, 128-symbol preamble, 1500\,\textmu s reply delay),
which would raise sweep rate to $\approx35$--45\,Hz.
All boards failed simultaneously, indicating a driver-level or SPI timing
incompatibility rather than a single faulty board.
The root cause is undiagnosed; we reverted to 110\,kbps / 5000\,\textmu s.

\textbf{Spatial error map lag.}
The Tier-2 correction $c_i(\hat{\mathbf{p}}_{t-1})$ uses the previous
sweep's EKF position (one-step lag, $\approx100$\,ms).
At walking speed this corresponds to $\approx10$\,cm of motion ---
negligible for the IDW correction accuracy.
At high speed or during abrupt turns, the correction lags by one sweep;
the linear fade at grid boundaries limits the impact.
