% ============================================================================
% Appendix B — Levenberg-Marquardt Multilateration
% ============================================================================

The residual for anchor $i$ at estimated position $\mathbf{p}$ is
$r_i(\mathbf{p}) = \|\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{a}_i\|_2 - \rho_i$.
The Jacobian row for anchor $i$ is
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{J}_i = \frac{(\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{a}_i)^\top}{\|\mathbf{p} - \mathbf{a}_i\|_2}
  \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times d}.
\end{equation}

The LM update at iteration $k$ is
\begin{equation}
  \Delta\mathbf{p} = -\bigl(\mathbf{J}^\top \mathbf{W} \mathbf{J}
    + \lambda \operatorname{diag}(\mathbf{J}^\top \mathbf{W} \mathbf{J} + 10^{-9}\mathbf{I})\bigr)^{-1}
    \mathbf{J}^\top \mathbf{W} \mathbf{r},
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{W} = \operatorname{diag}(w_i)$ and $\lambda$ is the
Marquardt damping parameter.
$\lambda$ is divided by 3 on a successful step and multiplied by 3 on failure,
bounded in $[10^{-9},\;10^9]$.
Convergence is declared when $\|\Delta\mathbf{p}\| < 10^{-6}$ or after 30 iterations.

The output covariance is the GLS approximation:
$\hat{\bm{\Sigma}} = \hat{\sigma}^2 (\mathbf{J}^\top \mathbf{W} \mathbf{J})^{-1}$,
where $\hat{\sigma}^2 = \sigma_{\text{range}}^2 = (0.10\,\text{m})^2$ is the assumed
range noise.
This covariance is used as the EKF measurement noise matrix $\mathbf{R}$.
