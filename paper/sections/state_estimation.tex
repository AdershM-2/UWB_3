\subsection{EKF Architecture and State}

All estimation runs on the host laptop; the rover MCU streams only raw ranges
(\textit{dumb-tag / smart-host} split).
Each tag has an independent FusionEKF.
The 6-dimensional state per tag is:
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{x} = \begin{bmatrix} p_x & p_y & v_x & v_y & b_x & b_y \end{bmatrix}^\top,
  \label{eq:state_vec}
\end{equation}
where $(p_x,p_y)$ is position, $(v_x,v_y)$ velocity, and $(b_x,b_y)$
accelerometer bias in the world frame.
$Z$-position is solved independently from 3D multilateration and fed as a
scalar sidecar observation.

\textbf{IMU-driven prediction.}
Linear acceleration from the BNO085 is rotated to world frame:
$\mathbf{a}_\mathcal{W} = \mathbf{R}(\mathbf{q})\mathbf{a}_\mathcal{B}$.
The discrete prediction over $\Delta t$ is:
\begin{align}
  p_\alpha' &= p_\alpha + v_\alpha\Delta t
    + \tfrac{1}{2}(a_\alpha - b_\alpha)\Delta t^2, \\
  v_\alpha' &= v_\alpha + (a_\alpha - b_\alpha)\Delta t,
  \quad b_\alpha' = b_\alpha,
\end{align}
for $\alpha \in \{x,y\}$.
Without IMU the predictor degenerates to constant-velocity
with inflated process noise ($\gamma^2 = 16$).
Singer model process noise:
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{Q} = \begin{bmatrix}
    \frac{\sigma_a^2 \Delta t^3}{3}\mathbf{I}_2 &
    \frac{\sigma_a^2 \Delta t^2}{2}\mathbf{I}_2 & \mathbf{0} \\[4pt]
    \frac{\sigma_a^2 \Delta t^2}{2}\mathbf{I}_2 &
    \sigma_a^2\Delta t\,\mathbf{I}_2 & \mathbf{0} \\[4pt]
    \mathbf{0} & \mathbf{0} & \sigma_b^2\Delta t\,\mathbf{I}_2
  \end{bmatrix},
  \label{eq:Qmatrix}
\end{equation}
with $\sigma_a = \SI{0.15}{\metre\per\second\squared}$ and
$\sigma_b = 10^{-3}\,\si{\metre\per\second\cubed}$.

\subsection{Adaptive Measurement Update}

The weighted LM fix $\hat{\mathbf{p}}_\text{ML}$ is treated as a direct
position observation: $\mathbf{H} = [\mathbf{I}_2\;\mathbf{0}_{2\times4}]$.
Measurement covariance is adaptively inflated:
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{R}_\text{eff} = \mathbf{R}_\text{base}\cdot
    \mathrm{clip}(\alpha_\text{rms}\cdot\alpha_\text{NLOS}\cdot\alpha_\text{DOP},\;1,\;50),
  \label{eq:Reff}
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{R}_\text{base} = \hat{\sigma}^2(\mathbf{J}^\top\mathbf{W}\mathbf{J})^{-1}$
from the LM solver, and:
\begin{align*}
  \alpha_\text{rms}  &= 1 + (\text{rms}/0.05)^2, \\
  \alpha_\text{NLOS} &= 1 + \mathrm{mean}_i\max(0,\,\text{gap}_i/6), \\
  \alpha_\text{DOP}  &= 1 + \max(0,\,(\mathrm{DOP}-0.10)/0.10).
\end{align*}
All three ranging-quality signals propagate into the EKF covariance,
so high-NLOS or high-residual fixes are trusted less.

\subsection{Robustness: Gating and ZUPT}

\textbf{NIS gate and divergence guard.}
Measurement updates are gated by the Normalised Innovation Squared:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{NIS} = \boldsymbol{\nu}^\top\mathbf{S}^{-1}\boldsymbol{\nu}
    \leq \chi^2_{2,0.95} = 5.991,
  \label{eq:nis}
\end{equation}
where $\boldsymbol{\nu} = \hat{\mathbf{p}}_\text{ML} - \mathbf{H}\mathbf{x}$
and $\mathbf{S} = \mathbf{H}\mathbf{P}\mathbf{H}^\top + \mathbf{R}_\text{eff}$.
After five consecutive rejections the filter reinitialises to
$\hat{\mathbf{p}}_\text{ML}$ with zero velocity.
An additional macro-divergence guard resets the filter if
$\|\hat{\mathbf{p}}_\text{EKF} - \hat{\mathbf{p}}_\text{ML}\| > \SI{2}{\metre}$
or $\mathrm{tr}(\mathbf{P}) > 25$.

\textbf{ZUPT.}
A sliding window of eight IMU samples triggers a zero-velocity
pseudo-measurement when the rover is stationary:
mean $|\mathbf{a}_{xy}| < \SI{0.10}{\metre\per\second\squared}$,
variance $< 0.005$\,(\si{\metre\per\second\squared})$^2$,
$|\omega_z| < \SI{0.087}{\radian\per\second}$, and
$|\mathbf{v}| < \SI{0.05}{\metre\per\second}$.
ZUPT drives velocity toward zero and corrects residual accelerometer offset
during stationary holds via the bias states.

\subsection{Full 6-DOF Output}

\textbf{Roll and pitch} are extracted from the BNO085 quaternion
$\mathbf{q} = [q_w, q_x, q_y, q_z]$:
\begin{align}
  \phi  &= \mathrm{atan2}(2(q_w q_x + q_y q_z),\; 1 - 2(q_x^2+q_y^2)), \\
  \theta &= \mathrm{asin}(\mathrm{clip}(2(q_w q_y - q_z q_x),\;{-1},{+1})),
\end{align}
at $\approx\SI{100}{\hertz}$ via the BNO085 Hillcrest fusion --- no
re-estimation is needed in the EKF.

\textbf{Yaw} is assembled from UWB dual-tag estimate
$\psi_\text{UWB}$ (Eq.~\ref{eq:dual_tag_yaw}) and BNO085 magnetometer-aided
yaw $\psi_\text{IMU}$.
$\psi_\text{IMU}$ corrects drift during straight-line motion;
$\psi_\text{UWB}$ anchors the estimate during turns.
\todo{Implement complementary filter or EKF fusing both yaw signals.}

The final position output is EMA-filtered:
$\hat{\mathbf{p}}_\text{out} = \alpha\hat{\mathbf{p}}_\text{EKF}
+ (1-\alpha)\hat{\mathbf{p}}_\text{out,prev}$
with $\alpha \in [0.15,\,0.50]$ (stationary to rover-motion preset).
Full 6-DOF pose $(\hat{p}_x, \hat{p}_y, \hat{p}_z, \hat{\phi}, \hat{\theta}, \hat{\psi})$
is streamed to the GUI and logged to CSV at every sweep.
